Springer Nature · Published August 27, 2026
A Multi-Objective Enterprise Readiness Framework for Serverless Retrieval-Augmented Generation
Amrin Yanya, Prof. Dr Talha Ali Khan, Raja Hashim Ali
Establishes the first systematic, quantitative benchmark for evaluating serverless LLM pipelines at enterprise scale. As AI moves from prototype to regulated production, organisations lack a principled way to compare RAG architectures across cost, reliability, and accuracy simultaneously. This work closes that gap. 200 configurations tested across FiQA, MS MARCO, and SQuAD 2.0 achieved a 77% reduction in hallucination rates at $2.93×10⁻⁷ per query. The Enterprise Readiness Score (ERS = 0.35×Accuracy + 0.25×CostEff + 0.25×CalibQual + 0.15×SLAComp) reached 72.7, with SLA compliance at 89.8% — providing practitioners with a replicable framework for production AI governance.
