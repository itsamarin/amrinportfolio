A Multi-Objective Enterprise Readiness Framework for Serverless Retrieval-Augmented Generation
Amrin Yanya, Prof. Dr Talha Ali Khan, Raja Hashim Ali
Introduces a systematic evaluation framework for serverless RAG architectures, balancing cost-efficiency, accuracy, latency, and SLA compliance. 200 configurations tested across FiQA, MS MARCO, and SQuAD 2.0 benchmarks yielded a 77% reduction in hallucination rates at $2.93×10−&sup7; per query. The composite Enterprise Readiness Score (ERS = 0.35×Accuracy + 0.25×CostEff + 0.25×CalibQual + 0.15×SLAComp) reached 72.7, with SLA compliance at 89.8%.
